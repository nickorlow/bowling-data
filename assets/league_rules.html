Winter 2027 League Rules

6 (or 5) teams will consist of three (or four) members each. Teams will play eachother once during the first 5 weeks of the season. Teams will be ranked going into the 6th week, and teams will play a tournament in the 6th week to determine the winner. The first and second seeded teams will get a bye round.

The normal USBC rules of bowling will apply to league games.

Schedule

Games will be played at 6:00pm on the following dates:

  • Wed. January 20th
  • Wed. January 27th
  • Wed. February 3rd
  • Wed. February 10th
  • Wed. February 17th
  • Wed. February 24th (Tournament)

Scorekeeping

Every week, each team will be paired against another team and they will each play three games. A team's score will be determined by taking the highest single game score for a team. The team with the higher score wins. In the event of a tie, a tie will be broken by (in order): second highest score, third highest score, total strike count, total spare count, and coin toss.

Tournament

In tournament play, teams will play one game per bracket level, and the team with the higher total score will advance. In the event of a tie, a tie will be broken by (in order): total strike count, total spare count, and a tiebreaker game played by 1 person from each team.

Absences

In the event of a player's absence, a fill in player selected by the team may play in place of the missing player. A team must have 3 players to play. Substitute players will(not)? count towards the individual statistics of a player for the purposes of prize allocation.
